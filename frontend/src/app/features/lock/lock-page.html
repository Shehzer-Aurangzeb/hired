h hired

A job posting in. A one-page CV out. Nothing invented on the way.

hired tailors a CV to a posting by picking from bullets you wrote yourself, then checks every claim against the boundary you set for that project.

  • Bullet ids, not prose

    The model returns ids from a fixed pool. It cannot write a claim that is not already in your profile.

  • Gaps surfaced, never papered over

    Anything the posting wants that no bullet covers comes back as a gap, not a stretched sentence.

  • One page, measured

    The renderer prints, counts pages and drops the lowest-priority bullet until it fits.
